# How a trial works

Each trial has the same steps, all in the middle of the screen:

1. A **cross** appears on a white panel. Look at the cross.
2. The **first diagram** flashes up for about 0.2 seconds.
3. A patch of **grey noise** flashes briefly, then the panel is blank for a moment.
4. The **second diagram** flashes up at the same place for about 0.2 seconds.
5. A question appears: *Which one had more items?*

Answer with the keyboard:

- press **F** or the **left arrow** if the **first** diagram had more items,
- press **J** or the **right arrow** if the **second** diagram had more items.

There is no time limit for your answer, and no other keys do anything. The trial advances by
itself as soon as you answer.

The diagrams consist of items (dots or small marks) connected by arrows. **Judge the number of
items, not the arrows or any outlines.** The two diagrams never contain the same number of items,
so always pick one, even when you are not sure. Please keep both hands on the keyboard.

Next you will do eight practice trials. In practice, and only in practice, we tell you whether you
were right.

Press **Enter** to continue.
