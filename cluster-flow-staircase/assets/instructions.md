# How a trial works

Each trial has the same five steps:

1. A **cross** appears in the middle of the screen, between two white panels. Look at the cross.
2. A diagram flashes up on **one side** (left or right) for about 0.2 seconds.
3. The screen goes blank for about 0.4 seconds.
4. A second diagram flashes up on the **other side** for about 0.2 seconds.
5. A question appears: *Which side had more items?*

Which side comes first changes from trial to trial, so keep your eyes on the cross.

Answer with the keyboard:

- press **F** or the **left arrow** if the diagram on the **left** had more items,
- press **J** or the **right arrow** if the diagram on the **right** had more items.

There is no time limit for your answer, and no other keys do anything. The trial advances by
itself as soon as you answer.

The diagrams consist of items (dots) connected by arrows. **Judge the number of items (the dots),
not the arrows.** The two diagrams never contain the same number of items, so always pick one,
even when you are not sure. Please keep both hands on the keyboard.

Next you will do eight practice trials. In practice, and only in practice, we tell you whether you
were right.

Press **Enter** to begin the practice trials.
