# Main task

Practice is over. The main task is one block of trials and takes about ten minutes. Every so often
a break page appears; rest as long as you need before going on.

Put your fingers on the **F** and **J** keys (or use the arrow keys) and keep your eyes on the
cross in the centre of the screen. **F** or **left arrow** means the **first** diagram had more
items, **J** or **right arrow** the **second**.

There is no feedback from now on. Answer with your first impression.

Press **Enter** when ready.
