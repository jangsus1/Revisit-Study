# Help

- **Correlation** ranges from 0 (no relationship) to 1 (points form a straight line). It measures how tightly the points cluster, not how steep the line is.
- **Camera**: the final task uses your webcam to estimate where you look. Video never leaves your browser; only on-screen gaze coordinates are saved.
- **Calibration dots**: look directly at each dot until it moves. Keep your head still and move only your eyes. Do not resize the browser window.
- If your face is not detected, improve the lighting on your face and sit about an arm's length from the screen.
