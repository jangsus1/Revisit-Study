# Task 3 - Instructions

In this final experiment, you will see scatterplots with two **real-world variables**. The variable labels appear after a short delay.

There are **12** tasks to solve after 2 example tasks. **Your webcam will estimate where you look** during this task.

## How It Works

### Step 0: **Camera setup and calibration** (once, before the task)
   - Enable your camera and make sure your face is detected.
   - Follow a dot with your eyes as it moves to 9 positions, then 5 more positions to check accuracy.
   - Keep your **head still** and move only your eyes.

### Step 1: **Quick calibration** (before every scatterplot)
   - Three dots appear one after another, then one dot in the centre. Look at each dot until it moves.

### Step 2: **Explore the scatterplot**
   - Click to start.
   - A scatterplot will be shown for **5 to 10 seconds**.
   - Observe the pattern and relationship between the points.
   - **The variable labels will appear after a few seconds and stay visible.**

### Step 3: **Estimate the Correlation**
   - After viewing the scatterplot, estimate the correlation with the slider (**0 to 1**).
