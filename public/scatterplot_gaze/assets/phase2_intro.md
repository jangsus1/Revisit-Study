# Task 3 - Instructions

In this final experiment, you will see scatterplots with two **real-world variables**. The labels may be shown from the start, after a few seconds, or never, and they may be blurred again before the plot disappears.

There are **24** tasks to solve after 2 example tasks. **Your webcam will estimate where you look** during this task.

> **Your camera video and your screen are never recorded or stored.** Only the estimated gaze position is saved.
>
> **Please keep your head as still as possible** from the calibration until the end of this task. Move only your eyes.

## How It Works

### Step 0: **Camera setup and calibration** (once, before the task)
   - Enable your camera and make sure your face is detected.
   - Look at a dot as it moves to 9 positions, then 5 more positions to check accuracy.
   - Keep your **head still** and move only your eyes. Please do not resize the browser window from here on.

### Step 1: **Quick check** (before every scatterplot)
   - A dot appears in the centre of the screen. Look at it until it disappears.
   - Sometimes a few more dots follow. Look at each one the same way.

### Step 2: **Explore the scatterplot**
   - Click to start.
   - A scatterplot will be shown for **5 to 11 seconds**.
   - Observe the pattern and relationship between the points.
   - **The labels may appear at any time, and may be blurred again before the end.**

### Step 3: **Estimate the Correlation**
   - After viewing the scatterplot, estimate the correlation with the slider (**0 to 1**).
